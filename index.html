About Us

Built by professionals who have performed the roles we support

Our team is built of professionals who have performed in the roles we support, delivering practical, mission-focused solutions grounded in real-world experience. Our consultants each bring decades of experience from frontline wildfire operations to executive-level leadership, with backgrounds spanning CAL FIRE, the U.S. Forest Service, Orange County Fire Authority, and the U.S. Coast Guard.

We work alongside agencies and organizations nationally and globally to strengthen operational readiness, modernize policy and training, and turn hard-won field experience into lasting institutional knowledge.

Our clients range from large publicly traded companies and government agencies to small businesses and individuals.

“Our team carries over 300 years of combined high-level experience, from the fireline to the executive suite.” Global Air Operations Group
