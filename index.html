About Us

Built by professionals who have performed the roles we support

Our team is built of professionals who have performed in the roles we support, delivering practical, mission-focused solutions grounded in real-world experience. Our consultants each bring decades of experience from frontline wildfire operations to executive-level leadership, with backgrounds spanning CAL FIRE, the U.S. Forest Service, Orange County Fire Authority, and the U.S. Coast Guard. That experience includes leading complex incidents, managing large-scale programs, and coordinating across multiple agencies in high-risk environments.

We work alongside agencies and organizations nationally and globally to strengthen operational readiness, modernize policy and training, and turn hard-won field experience into lasting institutional knowledge.

Our clients range from large publicly traded companies and government agencies to small businesses and individuals.
