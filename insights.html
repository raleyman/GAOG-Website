Wildfire, Aviation & Business Insights

Original analysis from our consultants, plus a running, curated watch on the industry news, reports, and business moves we think are worth your time.

Our Analysis Jul 1, 2026

Retardant Is Not Just for Airtankers Anymore

Why it matters: Our team dug into why ground-applied retardant is so underutilized despite being incredibly effective, and why it deserves a permanent place in initial attack, contingency planning, and prescribed fire doctrine, not just aerial delivery.

Business Highlight Lockheed Martin Newsroom Jan 26, 2026

Lockheed Martin, PG&E Corporation, Salesforce and Wells Fargo Launch EMBERPOINT to Transform America's Wildfire Prevention, Detection and Response

Worth a look: EMBERPOINT pairs Lockheed Martin's sensing and autonomous systems, Salesforce's Agentforce and Slack for real-time coordination, PG&E's on-the-ground mitigation experience, and Wells Fargo's capital behind one venture aimed at faster wildfire detection and response. Two of our own consultants, Aaron Burrough and John Messina, serve as advisors to the effort, a genuine business powerhouse worth keeping an eye on as it moves toward public demonstrations.

Blog Post The Hotshot Wake Up Aug 11, 2026

Big Move: Forest Service Consolidates National Wildfire and Aviation Authorities Under Its Deputy Chief for Fire and Aviation Management

Why it matters: The Forest Service just centralized wildfire and aviation decision-making, expenditure authority, resource allocation, and emergency declarations under one national Deputy Chief, with Geographic Area Fire Directors now reporting directly to that position instead of through regional leadership. Streamlining the chain of command like this can genuinely speed up decisions, but a lot of program knowledge and working relationships live at the regional level. It'll be interesting to watch how the agency navigates this consolidation, and the other changes still coming, over the next fire season.

Article FireRescue1 Aug 7, 2026

‘We have a problem’: NTSB report details final moments before Utah firefighting helicopter crash

Why it matters: The preliminary NTSB finding is that the helitanker “struck rising terrain” during a water mission. It's a sobering reminder that terrain awareness and mission-brief discipline can never become routine, and exactly the kind of finding that belongs in every program's next AAR, not just the operator involved.

Article Vertical Mag Aug 3, 2026

2 Killed in Mid-Air Helicopter Crash During Fire Fight in Greece

Why it matters: Cause is still under investigation, but a mid-air collision between two firefighting helicopters working the same fire is a hard reminder of what's at stake. Rigorous airspace deconfliction, air-to-air communication discipline, and multi-aircraft coordination training are never optional once more than one aircraft is working a fire.

Article NBC San Diego Jul 20, 2026

Cal Fire Reopens Its Ramona Air Attack Base With New Firefighting Resources

Why it matters: Members of our team had the privilege of attending the reopening of Cal Fire's Ramona Air Attack Base in person. Years in the making, the rebuild adds a new C-130H air tanker, an S-70 Firehawk helicopter, and a retardant mixing plant capable of holding up to 75,000 gallons. These are real gains in firefighting capability and initial attack effectiveness for San Diego County, and credit is due to Cal Fire, San Diego County, and Perimeter Solutions on a significant investment.

Article Lockheed Martin Newsroom Jul 14, 2026

Production of Europe's First Sikorsky FIREHAWK Helicopters Begins in Poland

Why it matters: The Firehawk's first deployment outside the U.S., built for the Czech Republic's RescEU program. Once one European operator stands up a Firehawk program, it becomes the template every other agency on the continent studies, including how they train and support it.

Article Governor of California Jun 4, 2026

Governor Newsom Announces Expansion of the World's Largest Civilian Aerial Firefighting Fleet

Why it matters: A fourth C-130H and an 11th helitack base for CAL FIRE. Worth watching how staffing, training, and program development keep pace with the hardware. That gap, not the aircraft count, is usually where a growing program actually struggles.

Article AerialFire Magazine Mar 12, 2026

Aviation Specialties Unlimited Recognizes E3 Early Adopters

Why it matters: Over 1,100 units sold and now expanding into Alpine operations. Night vision equipment decisions like this ripple straight into training curriculum and currency requirements for any program flying after dark.
