Privacy Policy

How we handle information collected through this website.

Effective date: August 29, 2026 · Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Who we are

Global Air Operations Group, LLC (“Global Air Operations Group,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a California limited liability company providing consulting, operational planning, training, and strategic support to organizations in the wildfire and emergency response sector.

This policy explains what information we collect through globalairoperations.com (the “Site”), why we collect it, and what choices you have. It covers only the Site. It does not cover information we receive in the course of a consulting engagement, which is governed by our contract with the client.

2. The short version

We built this Site to tell you who we are and let you reach us. Accordingly:

  • We use no cookies.
  • We use no analytics, tracking pixels, session recording, or advertising technology of any kind.
  • We have no user accounts and no logins.
  • We do not accept payments through the Site.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We never have and we do not intend to.
  • The only personal information we receive from the Site is what you choose to put in an email to us.

3. Information you provide to us

Our contact page presents a form, but it is not a hosted form. When you click “Send Message,” the Site does not transmit anything to us or store anything on our servers. Instead, your own email program opens with a pre-filled draft addressed to us, which you then choose whether to send. Nothing reaches us until you send that email.

When you do email us — through the contact page or directly — we receive whatever you include, typically:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your organization, if you provide it
  • The subject or service area of your inquiry
  • The content of your message and any attachments you send

If our work together progresses, you may also send us business contact details, program documents, or operational information. We treat all of it as confidential.

4. Information collected automatically

The Site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. However, like every website, the Site is delivered by a hosting provider that maintains standard server logs to keep the Site secure and running. Those logs may include your IP address, the date and time of your request, the page or file requested, referring page, and your browser type and operating system. We do not use these logs to build profiles, track individuals across sites, or identify you, and we do not combine them with any other information about you.

5. Service providers who may process information

We keep this list short deliberately. We share personal information only with the following categories of third parties, and only as needed to operate:

  • Website hosting. Our host delivers the Site and maintains the server logs described above.
  • Email and productivity services. Our business email and document services process and store the messages you send us, in the same way any business email provider does.
  • Web font provider. The Site loads typefaces from a third-party font service. Your browser's request for those fonts discloses your IP address to that provider. This is used only to deliver the fonts.
  • Professional advisors and legal compliance. We may disclose information to our accountants, insurers, or legal counsel where necessary, or where we are required to do so by law, subpoena, or valid legal process, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

We do not disclose personal information to advertisers, data brokers, marketing partners, or analytics companies, because we do not use any.

6. How we use information

We use the information you send us to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and correspond with you
  • Evaluate, scope, and deliver consulting services
  • Prepare proposals, agreements, invoices, and records
  • Maintain our business, professional, and accounting records
  • Comply with law and enforce our agreements

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, our legal bases for this processing are our legitimate interests in responding to inquiries and operating our consulting practice, the performance of a contract where we are engaged to provide services, your consent where you have voluntarily sent us information, and compliance with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

7. Do Not Track

Some browsers transmit a “Do Not Track” signal. Because the Site does not track visitors, use cookies, or employ any analytics or advertising technology, there is nothing for a Do Not Track signal to disable. Our practices are the same for all visitors regardless of whether such a signal is sent.

8. Third-party tracking on the Site

We do not permit any third party to collect personally identifiable information about your activity on the Site or across other websites over time. No advertising networks, analytics providers, or behavioral tracking services operate on the Site.

9. Links to other websites

Our Insights page and other pages link to third-party publications, agency announcements, and news coverage. Those sites are not under our control and have their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for their content or their handling of your information. Review their policies before providing them information.

10. How long we keep information

We keep email correspondence and client records for as long as needed for the purpose for which they were provided and thereafter as required for our business, tax, insurance, and legal record-keeping obligations. Server logs held by our hosting provider are retained on that provider's standard schedule, typically a short period measured in days or weeks. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or retain it in a form that is no longer readily identifiable.

11. How we protect information

The Site is served over encrypted HTTPS connections. Our email and document systems are protected by access controls, multi-factor authentication, and the security measures our providers maintain. Access to client information is limited to the consultants who need it for the engagement.

No method of transmitting information over the internet or by email is completely secure. Please do not send us sensitive personal information, security-sensitive operational details, or confidential agency material through an unencrypted email or the contact form. If you need to transmit something sensitive, contact us first and we will arrange an appropriate method.

12. Your choices and your rights

Everyone. You may ask us at any time what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email info@globalairoperations.com and we will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity before acting, and we may retain information where we have a legal or contractual obligation to do so. We will not discriminate against you for making a request.

California residents. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law, and we have not done so in the preceding twelve months. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes requiring a right to limit. We do not meet the thresholds that make us a “business” subject to the California Consumer Privacy Act, but we will honor requests to know, correct, or delete as described above. More information about California privacy rights is available from the California Attorney General.

Oregon residents. We do not meet the applicability thresholds of the Oregon Consumer Privacy Act, but we will honor requests to know, correct, or delete as described above, including a request for a list of the categories of third parties to whom we have disclosed personal data.

Residents of the EEA and the United Kingdom. Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise any of these rights, email us. You may also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority or, in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner's Office.

13. International visitors

We are located in the United States and our service providers process information in the United States. If you contact us from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, where data protection law may differ from the law of your country. Where we transfer personal data from the EEA or the United Kingdom, we rely on appropriate safeguards, including standard contractual clauses where required.

14. Children

The Site is a business-to-business professional services site and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or the law. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and post the revised policy here. If we make a material change to how we handle personal information already collected, we will take reasonable steps to notify affected individuals directly, including by email where we have an address. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
