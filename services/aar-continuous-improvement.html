Services

AAR & Continuous Improvement

Facilitated after-action reviews that turn a difficult incident into a clear, actionable path forward — led by consultants with command-level experience running the reviews themselves.

Every incident, successful or not, has lessons in it. Our facilitated after-action reviews (AARs) are built to surface those lessons honestly, without turning the process into a blame exercise, and to translate them into specific, trackable safety enhancements.

Our consultants have served in command and general staff roles — including Operations Section Chief and Type 1 Incident Commander — on significant incidents, so the reviews we facilitate come from firsthand knowledge of how decisions get made under pressure.

We don't stop at the report. We help organizations track follow-through on corrective actions so the lessons actually change how the next incident is run.

What's Included

  • Facilitated after-action reviews (AARs)
  • Lessons-learned documentation
  • Safety enhancement recommendations
  • Follow-through tracking on corrective actions
Includes consultants with Type 1 Incident Commander and Operations Section Chief Type 1 experience.
