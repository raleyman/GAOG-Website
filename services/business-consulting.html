Business Consulting

Considering starting a business in the fire and aviation space? We help vet the idea and get it off the ground.

The wildfire and emergency response industry rewards people who understand it from the inside — and that's exactly the perspective our consultants bring to a new venture. Whether you're evaluating a service, product, or contracting opportunity in the fire space, we help you pressure-test the idea before you commit resources to it.

That includes a candid look at where the concept is strong, where it needs work, and what it will actually take to operate in this industry — from regulatory and certification pathways to who the real decision-makers are.

We work with individuals and small teams at the earliest stages, when an experienced, honest second opinion is worth the most.

What's Included

  • Business concept and market vetting
  • Regulatory and certification pathway guidance
  • Startup planning and positioning
  • Introductions within the fire and aviation industry
