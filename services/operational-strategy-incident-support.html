Operational Strategy & Incident Support

Operational strategy and incident support from consultants who have held these roles in the field, not just studied them from the outside.

Wildfire and emergency incidents move fast, and the decisions made in the first hours often shape everything that follows. Our consultants bring aviation operations, fire chemical, communications, and strategy & tactics expertise built over careers in command roles, not as outside observers, but as people who have run these operations themselves.

We work with agencies and organizations before the season starts, helping shape operational plans, airspace coordination, and retardant and fire chemical programs, and we're available to support command staff during active incidents when an outside, experienced perspective is most valuable.

The goal is always the same: give decision-makers clear, field-tested options, not theory.

What's Included

  • Aviation operations planning and airspace coordination
  • Fire chemical and retardant program guidance
  • Incident communications architecture
  • Strategy and tactics review for complex incidents
Our consultants hold ICS aviation qualifications including Air Tactical Group Supervisor (ATGS), Air Operations Branch Director (AOBD), and Helicopter Coordinator.
