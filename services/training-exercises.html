Training & Exercises

Training designed and delivered by consultants who have taught these courses and led these positions themselves.

Readiness is built long before an incident starts. We design and deliver Aviation ICS training, field training assignments (FTAs), and tabletop and full-scale exercises tailored to your organization's specific operations and risk profile.

Because our consultants have served as instructors and evaluators in these same programs, training sessions are built around realistic scenarios and the judgment calls that actually come up in the field, not just a checklist of requirements.

We can build a standalone course, a full curriculum, or an ongoing exercise cycle, depending on what your team needs.

What's Included

  • Aviation ICS course design and delivery
  • Field training assignments (FTAs)
  • Tabletop and full-scale exercise design
  • Customized curriculum for agency-specific needs
Includes consultants with direct experience developing and instructing at programs such as the California Aerial Supervision Academy.
