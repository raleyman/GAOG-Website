Our Team

Decades of Frontline & Aviation Leadership

Our consultants bring decades of frontline wildfire and aviation experience to executive-level leadership.

Aaron Burrough

Principal

As CAL FIRE's Staff Chief of Aviation, Aaron led one of the world's largest firefighting aviation fleets and its shift to the Black Hawk helicopter.

John Messina

Principal

John rose from Unit Chief, serving as fire chief for Butte County and Paradise, to Assistant Region Chief over CAL FIRE's Northern Region.

Tim Perkins

Senior Consultant

Tim led Orange County Fire Authority's UAS program and Firehawk helicopter acquisition as Assistant Chief of Operations.

Mike Deacon

Senior Consultant

Mike serves as an Airborne Mission Commander for FIRIS, delivering real-time infrared and LiDAR intelligence to incident commanders statewide.

Jon Tishner

Senior Consultant

Jon led the Mendocino Hotshots and U.S. Forest Service aviation programs over 30 years, including large-scale prescribed fire and aerial ignition operations.

Shawn Raley

Senior Consultant

Shawn was a long-tenured Operations Section Chief Type 1 and Type 1 Incident Commander on some of California's largest, most complex fires.

Shawn Walters

Senior Consultant

Since 2014, Shawn has commanded the Pacific Southwest Region's Fire Watch Cobra and Night Watch platforms, after two decades in aerial supervision.

Travis Alexander

Senior Consultant

Travis rose from Firefighter I to Chief of CAL FIRE's Tactical Air Operations, leading the department's S-70i Firehawk and C-130 airtanker acquisition programs.

Jake Serrano

Senior Consultant

Jake led Air Attack and Helitack operations as Battalion Chief at Sonoma Air Attack Base, managing CAL FIRE's Type I helicopter programs.

Ray Taglio

Senior Consultant

Ray retired as Battalion Chief and Air Attack Base Manager at Ukiah, after building the Mendocino Unit's training and certification programs.

JJ Briggs

Senior Consultant

A Distinguished Flying Cross recipient, JJ served as Coast Guard Air Boss and standardized 33 pilots before turning to flying wildfire missions across the West.
